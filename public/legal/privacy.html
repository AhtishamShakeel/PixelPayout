1Who we are

PixelPayout is operated by (“we”, “us”), who is responsible for your personal information (the “controller”). This policy explains what the PixelPayout app and its website (the “App”) collect, why, and the choices you have. It should be read with our Terms of Service.

2Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your display name, email address and password. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We never see or store them in readable form.
  • Referral code you enter, if any.
  • Redemption details: the game you choose, and the player ID, in-game username and server you give us for delivery.
  • Support tickets and messages you send us in the App or by email, including the category you choose and any order you link.

Information collected automatically

  • Device identifiers: your device’s Android ID, which we use only to prevent multiple accounts and referral abuse, and your Advertising ID, which is used by our ad and offer partners.
  • Device and app information: device model, operating system version, app version, language and network information. Our providers may also receive your IP address and approximate location derived from it.
  • Activity in the App: games played and scores, quiz answers, XP, levels, Stars, daily streaks and goals, rewarded-ad claims, extra attempts, tournament standings, referral progress and your redemption history.
  • Diagnostics and usage: crash reports and app usage events, via Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics.

Information from partners

  • When you complete an offer, the offer partner tells us your PixelPayout user ID, the offer and the reward amount, so we can credit you.

We don’t collect your contacts, photos, precise GPS location, or payment or bank details.

Legal and safety

We may disclose information if the law requires it, to protect users or the App from fraud or harm, or as part of a sale or reorganisation of the App. If that happens, this policy continues to apply to your information.

We don’t sell your personal information for money. Letting ad partners use device identifiers for personalised advertising may count as “sharing” or “selling” under some US state laws. You can opt out as described in section 5.

5Ads and your choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: the first time you open the App, we ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Profile → Account → Privacy choices.
  • Everyone: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone’s Settings → Google → Ads. You will still see ads, but they will be less relevant.
  • Rewarded ads are always optional.

6International transfers

Our providers store and process data on servers in several countries, including the United States. Where data leaves the EEA or UK, it is protected by safeguards such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

7How long we keep it

  • While your account is open, we keep your account and activity data so the App works.
  • Support tickets are kept while your account is open, so you can look back at past conversations.
  • When you delete your account, your profile, activity, reward history, support tickets and sign-in account are deleted straight away. They disappear from backups within 30 days.
  • Kept for up to 2 years after deletion, then deleted:
    • redemption records (order details, player ID, username, server, email and display name), to handle disputes and payout questions;
    • anti-fraud records (your Android ID and a one-way hash of your email), to stop the same person re-claiming new-user and referral rewards;
    • offer-completion records received from partners;
    • per-game-account records used for the one-time first-redeem discount.
  • Analytics and crash data are kept according to Google Firebase’s own retention settings (up to 14 months for Analytics, and 90 days for Crashlytics).

8Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you, and get a copy;
  • correct information that is wrong;
  • delete your account and information. You can do this in the App, or see the deletion page;
  • object to or restrict some processing, and withdraw consent;
  • opt out of “sale”, “sharing” or targeted advertising (US state laws);
  • complain to your local data protection authority.

Email from the address on your account. We reply within 30 days, and may need to confirm your identity first. We won’t treat you differently for using these rights.

9Security

Data is encrypted in transit and stored on Google Cloud infrastructure. Access to your account data is restricted by security rules, and reward balances are only changed by our servers. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

10Children

PixelPayout is not intended for anyone under 18, and we don’t knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

11Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when the App changes, and change the effective date above. If a change is important, we will tell you in the App.
