Delete your PixelPayout account

PixelPayout, by

1In the app (fastest)

  1. Open PixelPayout and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

2Without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, email from the email address on your account, with the subject “Delete my account”. We delete the account within 7 days and confirm by email.

3What happens to your data

Deleted immediately

  • Your sign-in account, display name and email on your profile.
  • Your Stars balance, XP, level, streaks, goals, tournament progress and referral code.
  • Your reward history, game sessions, saved game account details and support tickets.

Kept for up to 2 years, then deleted

  • Redemption records (orders, player IDs, email), for payout disputes.
  • Anti-fraud records (device Android ID and a one-way hash of your email), to stop repeat claims of new-user and referral rewards.
  • Offer-completion records from partners, and first-redeem discount records per game account.

Deleting is permanent. Any Stars left on the account are lost and can’t be restored. If a redemption is still pending, wait for it to finish before deleting.
